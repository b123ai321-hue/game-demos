How to Play

  1. Reinforce: 3 troops/turn +1 per town you hold. Click your units (+1) or an empty square by your HQ or a town you hold (new Infantry, costs 2).
  2. Maneuver: click a unit, then green to march or red to attack. Each unit acts once per turn.
  3. Corps [G]: with Corps orders on, the whole corps (I, II, III, Reserve) marches together, and corps-mates touching the target add half their strength to an attack.
  4. Every attack is a question. You can attack only if attack beats defense. Right = victory (★★★ = biggest bonus + Glory). Wrong = lose troops, see the solution, turn ends.
  5. Flanking: 2+ of your units touching the enemy = +2 (cavalry +4). Infantry +1 def; cavalry moves 2; artillery fires from 2 squares, weak in melee. Redoubts (earthworks) give +3 defense.
  6. Win: take the enemy General's HQ or break their army (under 25%). Nightfall at turn 30: the stronger army wins.
  7. Armory [A]: spend Glory on formations (switch with F) and elite allies (equip up to 3). Skills [K]: spend ✪ skill points on three skill trees. Campaign [M]: conquer a wide map of Europe with armies, fleets and forts.

The Grand Campaign · Europe

Rules of the campaign
  1. March: select an army, click green squares (forest 2, mountains 3, rivers only at bridges). Step on cities, ports, passes and bridges to take them.
  2. Fight: red = attack. Choose a tactical battle [T] or a one-question quick battle [Q]. Forts and defended points need a siege: 1 correct answer per level.
  3. Reserve troops: [R] reinforce an army on your point · [P] raise an army at Paris · [B] build a defense on the cursor point (Redoubt → Fort → Fortress, +3 defense per level; enemies must stop next to it; port defenses fire on enemy fleets).
  4. Navy: [S] ships of the line or [F] frigates at your port. Fleets sail on sea squares; red = naval battle (a question). A fleet next to an enemy port blockades it: no troops from it, and the Coalition levy shrinks. Click your fleet beside an army to board; select the fleet and click a gold square to land.
  5. Take 5 capitals to win. Lose if Paris falls. Beware Britain's Royal Navy.

The Armory

Orders from the Emperor

  1. Reinforce: click your blue units to add troops (or press D to put them all on the cursor's unit).
  2. March: click a unit, then a green square. Cross the river only at bridges ═ and fords ≋. Redoubts (earthworks) give +3 defense.
  3. Attack: click a red-bordered enemy. Every attack is a Pre-Calc question; a wrong answer ends your turn.
  4. Team up: press G for Corps orders, so neighbors add strength. Two units touching an enemy = flank bonus.
  5. Study smart: check "Progress by Topic" and turn on Drill weak topic (W) to practice what you miss most. Spend Glory in the Armory (A).
  6. Skills (K): every 5 correct answers, each victory and each capital earns a ✪ skill point. Spend them on Infantry, Cavalry & Artillery, or Navy & Logistics.
  7. Campaign (M): armies march across Europe; fleets sail, carry armies, blockade ports and fight naval battles (every naval attack is a question too). Build defenses with B. Take 5 capitals.
  8. Take the enemy General (★ HQ, far right), break their army, or be stronger at nightfall. Press H to see this again.
