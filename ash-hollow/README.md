# Ash Hollow
Watch four people with no fire, tools or words invent a world. Open `index.html` (works offline from file://).
Sit back — the director camera follows what matters, and now and then glances at other peoples' wars, pacts and trade.
Space pause · 1–7 speed (1·2·4·8·16·25·50×) · C director · M world map (at 10,000 people; click a settlement to watch it) · V watch another people · H home · P optional predator · X a stranger who brings a breakthrough (at most once every 3,000 calendar years).
On the world map, I switches to Ideas & philosophies: peoples' intellect drifts over the ages, sharper peoples coin their own ideas, duller ones adopt them more readily, and ideas spread by contact like faith.
Late in history, industry can sicken the land, Nuclear Power can be turned into a weapon (nuclear winter follows), and a people that loses most of its folk can fall back ages and rebuild from scratch. These all emerge in play; none are scripted.
The world autosaves (localStorage) and resumes on reload; "✦ New world" starts over from 4 people.
`index.html?demo=world` fast-forwards to the world map (demo/test URLs never touch the save). See DESIGN.md.
