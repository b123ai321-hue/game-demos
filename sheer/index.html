Study modes: answer one question on each new ledge. Correct = full grip, full chalk, warmth. (Story uses the selected study mode too.)

How to climb

  • Hands: hold Left mouse (or Q) to lift your left hand, move the mouse onto a hold, release to grab. Right mouse (or E) does the right hand. Miss, and the hand goes back where it was.
  • Simple mode: point at a hold and press Space (or tap it). Your trailing hand reaches for it.
  • One hand always has to be holding on. Your feet find footholds by themselves. Dangling feet put more weight on your arms.
  • Grip (L/R bars) drains while you hang. Jugs are friendly, edges are fine, crimps and slopers are brutal, and snow makes them worse. A free hand shakes out and recovers.
  • C chalk: some grip back and dry hands for a while. You don't have much. Every new ledge gives +2.
  • Grab a ledge with both hands to pull onto it. On a ledge you rest and warm up. A/D walk, F places an anchor.
  • If both hands come off, you fall back to your last anchor. So place them.
  • Warmth drops with height, wind and snow. When you're cold your grip gets weaker. Gusts shake you, and they're worst when you're on one hand.
  • Esc/P pause, M mutes all sound and music (or use the Sound button). The Free climb route is 240 m to the summit.

Story & mountain travel

  • Cutscenes: Click / Space / Enter (or tap) shows the next panel or page. Esc skips the scene.
  • Walking: hold D / → to walk on, A / ← to step back. Hold Shift to run (it burns stamina fast). Walking uses a little stamina; stand still to get your breath back. Deep snow is slow and tiring. Warmth drops all the time, and faster in wind.
  • Knife-edge ridge: keep the balance needle in the middle. Q / Left mouse leans left, E / Right mouse leans right. Gusts and walking push you off centre.
  • Trek events (watch the prompt in the middle of the screen): Crevasse: Space jumps. Take off just before the gap. If you fall in, the rope pulls you back out. Rockfall: a shadow marks where it will land. Walk or run out from under it. Gust: when GUST COMING shows, hold S to brace until it passes. Ice slope: press Space when the shrinking ring meets the circle to kick in a crampon step. Mistime it and you slide: press S at SELF-ARREST to stop with your axe, or you slide back to the bottom. Avalanche: run (Shift+D) to the black boulder before the snow reaches you. Set pieces: dying headlamp, tap Space fast to swap batteries. Partner in a crevasse, Space on the ring to haul. Lightning, X drops your axe, then hold S+D to crawl. Choices, 1 or 2 (touch: left or right half of the screen). Choices change chapter endings. They get harder every chapter. K2 is the hardest.
  • Chapter goals: Ch1, top out the school-wall route before the clock runs out (or retry), then walk the night trek by headlamp cone. Ch2, when Iver slips, hold S before the countdown ends to catch him. Ch3, beat Dario to the col (the meter at the top shows ahead or behind; if he arrives first you retry, unless you stopped to help him). Ch4, thin air blurs and slows you, and warmth is your clock: reach the top before it runs out. Story walls differ by chapter; K2 has an ice section (pale wedges are axe placements) and an overhang where your feet don't help.
  • Camp (tent): walking past one is a checkpoint. It saves your progress and gives you some stamina and warmth back.
  • At the end of each trek a rock step switches to the normal two-hand climbing. Top out to finish the chapter.
  • Touch: hold the right half of the screen to walk and the left half to step back. On a ridge, hold the left or right third to lean and the middle to walk. Tap the top third to jump or kick an ice step. Hold the hint bar at the very bottom to brace or self-arrest. Hold the far right edge to run.
  • Progress is saved at the start of every stage and at every camp. Story → Continue picks up where you left off.
