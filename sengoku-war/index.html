法How to play

  • Select: left-click a counter · Shift-click to add · left-drag a box · double-click = all of that type on screen.
  • Orders: right-click ground to move (keeps formation) · right-click an enemy to attack · right-drag to draw a march path · Shift+right-click queues waypoints.
  • Keys: H hold · M merge same-type · S split · Ctrl+1–5 save group, 1–5 recall (twice = jump) · R recruit at your main castle · Space pause · + / − speed · wheel zoom · middle-drag, arrow keys or the minimap to pan.
  • Recruit: left-click one of your towns 町 or castles 城 to open its recruit panel. Rice 米 flows in from every place you hold. Towns train light troops; castles train everything.
  • Troops: 槍 spears beat cavalry · 侍 heavy samurai beat spears · 騎 cavalry crush 弓 archers, 鉄 guns and 砲 cannon · 僧 monks and spears stop cavalry · 鉄 teppo fire huge, slow volleys (weak in rain and melee) · 砲 cannon outrange everything and smash castle garrisons, but need an escort · 騎弓 mounted archers shoot on the move and back off from melee · 忍 ninja scout far, hide in forest, capture towns fast and cut enemy rice when inside their town.
  • Terrain & weather: plains fast · forest slow, +30% defense · hills +35% defense and longer range · rivers very slow except at fords/bridges · mountains impassable. You only see enemies near your troops and towns; rain and fog shorten sight.
  • Tactics: the notch on each counter shows where it faces. Engaged units turn slowly, so pin the enemy from the front, then hit the side (側 FLANK ×1.5) or rear (背 REAR! ×2.2, big morale shock). Units touched by 2+ enemies are 囲 surrounded and break fast. Guard your own flanks — enemy cavalry will try to get behind you.
  • Counters: the right troop type does about double damage (✓ Counter), the wrong one about half (✗). Cavalry charging spears or monks head-on is 槍 repelled; a 突 charge into a flank or rear does ×2.5 and stuns for 2 s.
  • Ground: 止 Hold (H) gives +30% defense to the front only. Attacking uphill deals ×0.75; hills add archer range. Forest gives ×1.5 cover from arrows and shot, and troops waiting unseen in forest strike first: 伏 AMBUSH ×2 for 3 s.
  • Morale: keep your 将 commander alive. Broken units flee (崩) and rally later.
  • Win: take the enemy's main castle or break their army (Okehazama: slay their commander). Lose if your main castle falls.
